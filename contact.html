Questions, answered

Frequently asked

What does "A2 desi cow milk" actually mean?

Indigenous Indian cow breeds — Gir, Sahiwal, Tharparkar — produce milk with only the A2 beta-casein protein. Most modern cross-breed and HF cows produce A1, which a growing body of research links to digestive discomfort in many people.

We only keep indigenous breeds. Every drop of milk we sell is A2 by birth, not by selection.

How is your bilona ghee different from regular ghee?

Bilona is the traditional method: milk is first set into curd, the curd is churned by hand to extract makhan (white butter), and only then is the makhan slow-simmered into ghee. Most commercial ghee skips straight from cream to ghee via centrifuge — faster, cheaper, but a different product.

We do bilona because our grandmothers did. The aroma and digestibility difference is something you taste, not argue.

How does cow adoption work?

For ₹4,999 a month you adopt one cow by name. You receive approximately 15L of her milk monthly, a welfare update with photos, and an invitation to visit her every quarter. The remaining funds cover her fodder, vet care and shelter.

Adoption is for a minimum of three months and can be paused or cancelled any time after.

Can I visit the gaushala with my family?

Yes — we welcome visitors every Saturday and Sunday, 8–11 am and 4–6 pm. Please book in advance through the form above or on WhatsApp, so we can prepare a small tour. Children are very welcome.

What is your refund or return policy?

For perishable items (milk, paneer, dahi) we replace any damaged or spoilt delivery on the next day, no questions asked. Non-perishable items (ghee, sweets, panchgavya) can be returned within 7 days if unopened.

Subscriptions can be paused, skipped or cancelled any time from your dashboard.
